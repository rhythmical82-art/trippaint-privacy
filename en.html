TripPaint Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 8, 2026

TripPaint (“the App”) is an Android application for organizing travel photos, locations, and travel records and for providing map, timeline, album, diary, and PDF features. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed by the App and by third-party services used by the App.

1. Information Processed by the App

When a user uses a feature or grants permission, the App may process the following information on the device:

  • Current location and travel route locations
  • Photos and videos selected by the user
  • Capture time, location data (including EXIF), and other media metadata
  • Trip titles, dates, places, participants, notes, diaries, and other user-entered content
  • Place names, addresses, coordinates, and map-related information
  • App preferences, language, PDF settings, and local synchronization information

Travel data is primarily stored locally on the user's device. TripPaint does not operate its own user-account system and does not directly collect email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or payment information for account registration.

2. Location Information

The App may request location permission to provide current-location display, maps, place recording, and travel-route features. If a user-selected photo contains location metadata, the App may use it for travel places and map display.

When map, place search, or route calculation features are used, coordinates, place queries, or related request information may be transmitted to Google services such as Google Maps, Google Places, and Google Routes.

3. Photos and Videos

Photos and videos explicitly selected by the user may be used for travel records, timelines, albums, maps, and PDF creation. TripPaint does not automatically upload user photos or videos to a server operated by TripPaint.

Removing a photo from a TripPaint trip does not delete the original photo or video stored in the device gallery, MediaStore, or other external storage.

4. Nearby Sharing and Travel Files

When the user explicitly initiates nearby transfer or travel-file export, information associated with the selected trip may be transferred to a destination chosen by the user, including:

  • Trip title, dates, participants, and notes
  • Place names, addresses, coordinates, and travel routes
  • Travel diaries and photo notes
  • Selected photos, videos, and related metadata

These transfers occur only when initiated by the user.

5. Advertising and Advertising-Related Data

TripPaint uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner advertisements on certain screens.

For ad serving, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention, Google Mobile Ads SDK and advertising technology providers may process information including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the device's general location
  • App launches, taps, and interactions with ads or content
  • Diagnostic information related to App and SDK performance
  • Android advertising ID, App Set ID, and other device or account-related identifiers

Data transmitted by Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Use of advertising identifiers may vary depending on device settings, consent status, and App or SDK configuration.

6. Advertising Consent Management

For users in regions such as the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) and a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) to request consent or present privacy choices related to advertising data processing.

Users may be able to consent, decline, or manage detailed options through the consent message and, where required, revisit their privacy choices within the App.

7. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services for functionality, security, advertising, and reliability:

  • Google Maps SDK
  • Google Places SDK
  • Google Routes API
  • Google Play services and location services
  • Nearby Connections
  • Firebase App Check
  • Google Play Integrity
  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)
  • ML Kit

These services may process IP addresses, device and app information, SDK identifiers, diagnostics, request data, location information required for a feature, or other information necessary for service delivery, security, advertising, analytics, and reliability.

8. Artificial Intelligence Features

AI travel diary functionality is not currently enabled for general users in the released version. During normal use, travel records and original photos are not automatically transmitted to an AI service.

If AI functionality is made available to users in the future, TripPaint will provide information about the data transmitted and the purposes of processing and will update this Privacy Policy where appropriate.

9. Data Storage and Android Backup

Travel information and related data are primarily stored on the user's device. If Android backup is enabled, the travel database and certain App preferences may be backed up and restored through Android's backup functionality.

Cache files, temporary PDF and transfer files, and certain device-specific synchronization information may be excluded from the App's backup configuration.

10. Data Deletion

Users can delete trips, places, and photo associations through App features. Removing a photo from a trip does not delete the original photo or video from the device.

When the App is uninstalled, locally stored App data is removed in accordance with Android operating-system policies. If Android backup contains previously backed-up App data, some information may be restored after reinstallation.

11. Sale of Data and Advertising Use

TripPaint does not directly sell user data. In connection with advertising, Google AdMob and related advertising technology providers may process data for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention.

12. Security

Communications with external services use encrypted connections such as HTTPS/TLS. TripPaint applies reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect user information.

13. Children's Privacy

TripPaint is not designed for the purpose of intentionally collecting children's personal information. If the App's target-age settings or applicability of Google Play Families policies changes, the App's policy and advertising settings will be updated as required.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when App functionality, advertising or analytics services, applicable law, or Google Play requirements change. Material changes may be communicated through the App or its distribution page.
